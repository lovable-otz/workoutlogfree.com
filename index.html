Why trust these numbers

Two kinds of numbers appear on this site. The first come out of your own log: session volume is the sum of weight × reps across your sets, and estimated one-rep max uses the Epley formula, weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). Both are arithmetic you can check by hand, and both are covered by automated tests that run against fixed worked examples before anything is published. The second kind are training guidelines, and those are quoted from the CDC physical activity guidelines for adults rather than paraphrased from other fitness sites. Where a figure appears, the source is linked next to it. Nothing here is medical advice, and no claim is made about what any product or programme will do for you. If you find an error, the contact page reaches us.

Common questions

What is the 3-3-3 rule for workout?

It is an informal phrase, not a published guideline, and it is usually given as three exercises, three sets, three days a week. No governing body defines it, so treat any confident-sounding version with suspicion. More in How to Keep a Workout Log.

Can my phone track my exercise?

Partly. A modern phone counts steps and estimates walking, running and cycling from its motion sensors, and the built-in health app will store that automatically. More in How to Keep a Workout Log.

What is the 3-2-1 rule for working out?

It is a social-media training template, commonly described as three strength sessions, two Pilates or mobility sessions and one cardio session per week, with a rest day. More in How to Keep a Workout Log.

What are the best devices to track workouts?

It depends what you are tracking. Watches and phones handle time, distance and heart rate for running, walking and cycling without any input from you. More in How to Keep a Workout Log.

What is the 3-3-3 rule gym?

In a gym context it is usually quoted as three exercises, three sets of each, three times a week. More in Free Workout Planner.

What is the best free workout planner?

The best free planner is whichever one you can change in ten seconds when the squat rack is busy. Rigid apps that lock the plan behind a paid tier fail that test. More in Free Workout Planner.
