Workout Log Free

Workout Log Free is a browser-based workout log and weekly planner. There is no app to install, no account and no server: your sets, reps and weights are saved in this browser and exported as CSV whenever you want a copy. The tracker totals volume and estimates one-rep max; the planner builds a printable split.

Why trust these numbers

Two kinds of numbers appear on this site. The first come out of your own log: session volume is the sum of weight × reps across your sets, and estimated one-rep max uses the Epley formula, weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). Both are arithmetic you can check by hand, and both are covered by automated tests that run against fixed worked examples before anything is published. The second kind are training guidelines, and those are quoted from the CDC physical activity guidelines for adults rather than paraphrased from other fitness sites. Where a figure appears, the source is linked next to it. Nothing here is medical advice, and no claim is made about what any product or programme will do for you.
